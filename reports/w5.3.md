There are little/big-endian small vfonts and a dense 256-glyph vfont; small and dense 224-glyph FZX; small and dense 358-glyph FIGlet. Official FIGlet 2.2.5 (BSD-3-Clause) and `chkfont` were built as external oracles from the upstream tagged tarball; no implementation was copied. ## Remaining format limitations

vfont retains one legacy byte range and has i8 bearings, i16 advances/per-glyph lengths and a u16 raster section. FZX has one byte range starting at 32, one global tracking value and 14-bit relative pointers; normalized width above 16, left bearing below -3, glyph top above the cell, inconsistent inferred tracking and unrepresentable offsets fail explicitly. FIGlet's bitmap model does not implement character-shaped art, smushing or hardblank barriers, and UTF-8 art is interpreted as original bytes rather than guessed Unicode columns. Canonical FIGlet emits `#`, fills required missing slots and rejects clipping; all resulting changes are Loss. Full original bytes remain model provenance, but writers never reuse them blindly after edits. These are documented representational boundaries, not hidden successful conversions.

Suggested coordinator decision entry: preserve raw FIGlet art and signed identities while explicitly projecting every non-space byte to ink; canonical writes use `#` and full-size layout per PLAN. FZX uses the specification's per-entry offsets and unsigned tracking; zero-area source dimensions are preserved natively even when the independent oracle collapses them. All three writers audit an actual same-limit reread so source metadata, aliases, order and derived metrics cannot disappear silently.
