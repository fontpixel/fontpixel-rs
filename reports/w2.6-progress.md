programs, hints, subroutines and CID dictionaries are retained. The first full
check.sh passed, and the first full Tong matrix passed all four outline/shape

## Third checkpoint: preservation review fixes validated

TrueType subsets now retain raw glyph records and patch composite IDs only,

needed relocation; it is fixed and independently accepted by OTS. External
Tong/FontTools GSUB with NULL DFLT DefaultLangSys is repaired to the mandatory
empty default, with an explicit repair diagnostic and unchanged shaping.

Sixteen focused Rust tests and nine additional independent external cases pass.

executions, each 611 seconds), but precede these last changes. Their logs remain
under pre-preservation and are not claimed as final. The first parity run had
