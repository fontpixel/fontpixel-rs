## Review findings and resolution

The candidate's extensive rendering checks did not establish every mandatory
packaging detail. Three reproducible conformance gaps were corrected before
integration, rather than accepted as limitations:

1. WOFF1 source table and whole-font checksum errors were normalized. Encoding now
   rejects them before packaging, following the explicit requirement in
   [WOFF1 §5](https://www.w3.org/TR/WOFF/#TableDirectory). That section also has
   later advisory wording about correcting checksums; the strict rejection rule
   is the chosen encoding boundary. Checksum-valid structural normalization
   retains diagnostics and removes any invalidated DSIG.
2. WOFF1 decode accepted an unpadded last table. The reader now requires all of
   its final zero padding. This check is distinct from WOFF2, whose final
   compressed block may legally be unpadded.
3. CFF and null-transform WOFF2 output omitted the required head.flags bit 11.
   Every output now sets it, as required by
   [WOFF2 §5](https://www.w3.org/TR/WOFF2/#table_format). The null-transform
   exact-byte regression constructs precisely the mandated head change and
   checksum update; all other bytes remain exact.

Incoming WOFF1 checksum-adjustment repairs also now get an explicit Repair
entry whether or not DSIG exists. The physical-table-order and retained-signature
regression remains exact. No glyph, bitmap, shaping or external-tool assertion
was relaxed. Original candidate code and raw evidence remain on its branch.
`w2.5-review/reproduced-candidate.txt` records minimal reproductions.

## Scope, resource limits, and next work item

Accepted W2.5 scope is standalone supported-sfnt ↔ WOFF1/WOFF2 coding. Physical
WOFF1 order, DSIG handling, transformed glyf/loca and optional hmtx, null forms,
auxiliary transport, actual decoded-byte/work limits and aggregate batch bounds
were reviewed. Parallel compressor working memory still scales with the chosen
worker product; metadata is opaque, not schema-validated, and signatures are
not cryptographically verified.
